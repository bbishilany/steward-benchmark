Channel 1

Why?

Three legs of organic growth

Your serious competitors are growing on three legs. Steward Partners has two working — the third is the opportunity this benchmark measures.

Referrals

Working. Doesn’t scale predictably and there’s no lever to pull when volume slows.

Not built yet. Creative Planning, Mariner Wealth, and Hightower all have location pages, service content, and directory infrastructure in every market they serve. This is the only leg that compounds without adding headcount.

This benchmark measures the size of that third-leg gap across 5 markets.

07 — Recommended Build

Recommended Architecture

The proposed location hierarchy gives every Steward Partners office a dedicated, indexable presence on the primary domain.

Corporate Hub
/locations/
→
National directory of all 80+ offices. Internal link equity hub. Targets “Steward Partners offices” queries.
State Pages
/locations/texas/
→
Rollup page per state. Aggregates offices, passes authority down. Targets “financial advisor [state]” terms.
Office Pages
/locations/texas/dallas/
→
Dedicated page per office with team, address, services. Primary ranking target for “financial advisor [city]”.
Service + City
/locations/texas/dallas/wealth-management/
→
Service-specific pages per city. Targets high-converting “[service] [city]” keywords.
Subdomain Migration Path

Existing team subdomains coexist during transition. Build the /locations/ hierarchy first, then redirect subdomains to their corresponding office pages over time. Preserves existing equity while consolidating future authority under the primary domain.
